HOW IT WORKS

Transparent by design

1

Regular season

Each team gets an 82-game result drawn from its adjustable strength rating and conference environment.

2

Postseason field

Teams are seeded by simulated wins. Optional play-in uncertainty gives seeds 7–10 a realistic shot.

3

Playoff bracket

Best-of-seven series use team strength, home advantage and your volatility setting.

4

Monte Carlo totals

The dashboard repeats the process and reports playoff, Finals and championship frequencies.

Predictions are scenario estimates, not betting advice. Default ratings are an editable illustrative preseason baseline, not live official standings or injury data.
